SIGTURK organizes a workshop series on Natural Language Processing for Turkic
Languages, co-located with major \*ACL conferences. The workshops bring together
researchers working on computational linguistics for Turkic languages. Below
you can find the pages, shared tasks and published proceedings of each edition.
