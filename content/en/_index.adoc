SIGTURK is the ACL Special Interest Group on Turkic Languages and aims to bring together all researchers working on computational linguistics in Turkic languages.

== Workshops

SIGTURK organizes a workshop series on Natural Language Processing for Turkic Languages, co-located with major *ACL conferences.
See the link:/workshops/[Workshops] page for all editions, their shared tasks and proceedings.
